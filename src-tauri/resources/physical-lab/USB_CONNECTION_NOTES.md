# USB connection notes for the photographed UNO-style board

For an Arduino UNO R3-style board, the board-side connector is normally USB Type-B
(the large, almost-square "printer cable" connector).

Many modern MacBook Air models have USB-C/Thunderbolt ports and no USB-A port.

Typical solutions:
1. USB-C to USB-B DATA cable
2. USB-C hub/adapter + existing USB-A to USB-B DATA cable

Do not force a connector that does not fit.
USB connectors are keyed and should insert with modest resistance when aligned correctly.

A 3–4 year old cable can be worn, but age alone is not usually the reason a connector
will not physically fit. More common causes:
- wrong connector type
- wrong orientation
- bent/damaged plug
- debris or damage in the port
- charge-only cable (fits but cannot transfer data)

Use only low-voltage USB-powered Arduino/sensor setups for the initial tests.
