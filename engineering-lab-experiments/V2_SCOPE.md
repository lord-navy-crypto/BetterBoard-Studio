# Engineering Lab v2 Scope

In scope:

- stronger measurement observability;
- validity and quality evidence;
- timing and synchronization evidence;
- calibration/configuration provenance;
- reproducibility metadata;
- auditable deterministic derivations;
- schema and compatibility verification;
- experiment-specific acquisition diagnostics.

Out of scope for firmware:

- declaring agreement with a physical model;
- automatic uncertainty conclusions;
- deciding that dynamics are chaotic;
- fitting scientific parameters as authoritative results;
- hiding poor measurements through opaque filtering.

This scope keeps BetterBoard focused on trustworthy acquisition while allowing Engineering Lab to remain the place where measurements and models are actually compared.
